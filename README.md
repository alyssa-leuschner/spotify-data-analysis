# Spotify Data Analysis

An analysis of my personal Spotify listening habits from January 2018 to July 2026.
Based on a dataset of nearly 90,000 streaming records, this project explores the evolution of my music taste over the past 8.5 years. It investigates if/ how major life events, such as moving to a new country or switching to a new field of study (from Humanities to Data Science), influenced my listening behaviour.
